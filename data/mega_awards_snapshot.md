## How to read “where they are sitting”

1. **Recipient location** = legal entity address / UEI registration (often HQ or contracting office).
2. **Place of performance** = where the work is actually done; can differ from HQ (e.g. MD PoP for a national healthcare network).
3. **MAC / IDIQ** winners “sit” on a vehicle until a task order funds real work.
4. Modifications (`mod`) mean the original award is still the home record; status is “active + changed value or POP.”
